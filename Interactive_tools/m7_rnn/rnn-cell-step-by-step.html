Slide α and you are changing both weights at once. An RNN learns its weights separately for every slot, which is why it needs far more data than smoothing does. To explore smoothing on its own, open the simple exponential smoothing tool.

1 · The state is a short list of numbers

Think of each number as a slot of memory. One slot might follow the recent level, one the trend, one the season. Nobody assigns those jobs. Training does.

2 · One formula, used at every step

New state = tanh(W × input + U × old state + bias). The same weights are used for month 1 and month 12. That is why an RNN can read a short window or a long one with the same number of weights.

3 · Big numbers freeze the state

tanh is flat beyond about 3. A raw count like 112, times an ordinary weight, lands far out on the flat part, so every slot answers 1 or −1 whatever the month. Rescale the data, always, with the mean and standard deviation of the training data only.

4 · A trend is a second problem

A state made of numbers between −1 and 1 does not reach far beyond what it saw in training, and the test year is higher than every training year. Taking logs and differences removes the trend, and the same network becomes a good forecaster. Next: how a dense network sees the same data.

Data: Box and Jenkins monthly international airline passengers, January 1949 to December 1960, thousands. The network is a simple RNN with 16 units (tanh) and one output unit, 305 weights, reading 12 time steps with 1 feature each, trained on the months before 1960 and tested on the 12 months of 1960, always one month ahead from real data. It was trained three times, once for each kind of input: raw counts, counts rescaled to mean 0 and standard deviation 1 (using the training months only), and the monthly change in the logarithm. Each version shown here is the run with the middle test error out of 10. Their weights are stored in this page and the forecasts are computed in your browser. Errors are mean absolute percentage errors. Explained in Deep Forecasting, Module 7.
