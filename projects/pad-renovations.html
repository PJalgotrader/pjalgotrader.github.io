§ 01 — The premise

The business

Selling the finished job

Lead sites sell the introduction and software vendors sell the toolbox. PAD sells the finished job. A homeowner makes one call, gets one fair price, and deals with one company that stands behind the work, while vetted local pros compete for the job behind the scenes in a transparent bid. It starts with painting on the Wasatch Front and grows trade by trade: HVAC, roofing, plumbing.

The moat is not the AI, which already exists. It is distribution, the service layer, a single-party guarantee, and the relationship with a homeowner across every trade in the house.

The software

An operating layer, not a CRM

An AI receptionist answers every call around the clock, introduces itself, answers questions and books the estimate on the spot, then texts back anyone who did not book. Quotes get automated follow-ups at one, three and seven days, drafted by Claude and sent by SMS, and cancelled the moment a lead closes.

A pipeline tracks every lead from first contact to won or lost, transparent bids price each job, a payments ledger tracks deposits and lien waivers, and a dashboard turns it all into the case-study numbers: missed-call rate, time to quote, close rate.
