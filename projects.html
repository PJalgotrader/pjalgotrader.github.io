iPhone app · App Store, 2026 · eisenhour.app

Eisenhour

Eisenhower matrix, by the hour. Most to-do apps make lists; Eisenhour makes decisions, then turns them into hours on a real calendar and shows you where your life actually goes. Four quadrants, time blocking beside the calendars already on your phone, a focus timer with a year-long heatmap, weekly targets per life area, and a business card you can share as an image or PDF.

Local-first by design: no account, no server, no tracking. The App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected” because there is nothing to collect. Free, with a one-time Pro unlock and no subscription.

Company · PAD Renovations LLC, Utah, 2026 · founder

PAD Renovations

An AI operating layer for residential contracting, and my own company. The premise in one line: lead sites sell the introduction and software vendors sell the toolbox; PAD sells the finished job. A homeowner makes one call, gets one price, and deals with one company that stands behind the work, while vetted local pros compete for the job behind the scenes. It starts with painting on the Wasatch Front and grows trade by trade.

The software runs the business. An AI receptionist answers every call around the clock, introduces itself, answers questions and books the estimate on the spot, then texts back anyone who did not book. Quotes get automated follow-ups at one, three and seven days, drafted by Claude and sent by SMS, and cancelled the moment a lead closes. A pipeline tracks every lead from first contact to won or lost, transparent bids price each job, and a dashboard turns it all into the case-study numbers: missed-call rate, time to quote, close rate.

Research tool · paper trading · not investment advice

Hunt Ledger

A paper-trading book for a monthly research exercise I call the “10×-by-2030 hunt”: which small companies have an arithmetically possible path to ten times their value by 2030, and what would have to be true for it to happen? The ledger starts with a notional $100,000 on August 21, 2026, imports one CSV of names and integer weights each month, sells what dropped off, rebalances the rest to target, records every fill, and marks the book against a $100,000 buy of SPY on the same day.

The part that matters is the process, not the tickers. Every name has to survive a bridge before it earns a weight: revenue to margins, margins to cash and capex, cash to financing and dilution, a deliberately compressed terminal multiple, and only then an equity value per share, in a base and a bull case, compared to the 10× price in plain words. Names are ranked on the quality of that path, never on how loud they are online, and each carries one sentence that would falsify the thesis. Most candidates fail the bridge, which is the point.

§ 02 — For students · Analytics Solutions Center

Web app · Huntsman School, Data Analytics & Information Systems

DAIS Resume Studio

AI-assisted resume coaching for USU’s DAIS students, built from the department’s own feedback protocol so the advice matches what instructors and the Huntsman career team actually say. Upload a resume and get strengths, ranked fixes and before-and-after bullet rewrites; paste a job posting for an honest gap analysis; turn a course project into bullets that only claim what you did; or restructure the whole document into the DAIS template and download it as Word.

An instructor mode keeps a human in the loop: the AI pre-flags issues from the shared comment library, the instructor decides what applies, and a ready-to-send letter comes out in the department’s format. Files are parsed in the browser and nothing is stored on a server.
